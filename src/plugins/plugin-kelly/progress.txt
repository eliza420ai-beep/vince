SECTION 1 — PROGRESS (what is done)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Plugin structure
  - plugin-kelly with services (KellyLifestyleService), actions (16), evaluators (1), providers (2), tasks (4).
  - Init runs only when character name is Kelly.

Actions (16)
  - KELLY_DAILY_BRIEFING: day-aware picks (health, dining, hotels) from the-good-life and curated-open-schedule.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_PLACE: one best pick + one alternative for hotel or restaurant; "eat in X", "wanna go eat"; place-aware retrieval; open today and Landes favorites for SW France; allowlist guard.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_WINE: one pick + alternative, SW France/Bordeaux default, tasting note and service; South African, whiskey, Armagnac when in wine-tasting knowledge.
  - KELLY_SURF_FORECAST: Biarritz wave height, period, direction, sea temp; interpretation with surf-ocean voice tint; rain/storm suggests indoor or surfer yoga.
  - KELLY_ITINERARY: multi-day trip plans (hotel, lunch, dinner per day) from the-good-life; travel/roadtrips, luxury-hotels.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_WORKOUT: one concrete workout (pool, gym, surfer yoga, swim) from pool/gym season and swimming context.
  - KELLY_WEEK_AHEAD: 3–5 suggestions for the week (dining, hotels, wellness) from the-good-life and curated schedule.
  - KELLY_SWIMMING_TIPS: tips for daily 1000m, winter pools, swimmer yoga from swimming-daily-winter-pools and yoga-vinyasa-surfers-swimmers.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_EXPERIENCE: wine tasting, spa, wellness, cooking class, guided tour; one pick + alternative; lifestyle/spa-wellness-guide, experience-prioritization-framework.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_HOME_COOKING: Green Egg, Thermomix, oven; dinner at home; wine pairing; lifestyle/home-cooking, local meat.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_TEA: Dammann Frères by time of day; one pick + alternative.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_ENTERTAINMENT: books, music, Netflix, Apple TV; lifestyle/entertainment-tastes.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_CREATIVE: oil painting, Hasselblad/Capture One, Ableton, Blackmagic/Resolve, Blender; creative-production/*.
  - KELLY_RECOMMEND_ROWING: water rower for surf/swim fit; lifestyle/water-rowing-surf-swim-fit; one session + benefit.
  - KELLY_INTERESTING_QUESTION: one thought-provoking question from lifestyle/interesting-questions.
  - REPLY / ASK_AGENT as used by character.
  - All use the-good-life knowledge and curated-open-schedule where applicable.

Providers (2)
  - kellyContext: today's wellness tip, day of week, season (pool/gym), restaurants open today (SW France/Landes), location context, yoga line, winter swimming line (gym season), known preferences from facts.
  - weather: Bordeaux + Biarritz conditions; marine/surf data for Biarritz (wave height, period, direction, sea temp); high-wind caution; cached 30 min; no beach/surf/outdoor dining in rain or storm.

Evaluator
  - lifestyleFeedback: captures lifestyle preferences from conversation for future personalization.

Tasks (4)
  - Daily lifestyle briefing push to channels whose name contains "kelly" or "lifestyle" (KELLY_LIFESTYLE_DAILY_ENABLED, KELLY_LIFESTYLE_HOUR).
  - Optional nudge task (KELLY_NUDGE_ENABLED, KELLY_NUDGE_DAY, KELLY_NUDGE_HOUR) to channels with "lifestyle" in name.
  - Optional weekly digest (KELLY_WEEKLY_DIGEST_ENABLED, KELLY_WEEKLY_DIGEST_HOUR).
  - Optional winter swim reminder (KELLY_WINTER_SWIM_REMINDER_ENABLED, KELLY_WINTER_SWIM_REMINDER_WEEK).

Knowledge (the-good-life) used
  - curated-open-schedule.md: restaurants by day, hotels by season, palace close/reopen, fitness/health (pool season, gym season).
  - lifestyle/: swimming-daily-winter-pools, surf-ocean-voice, wellness-reminders, yoga-*, tea-dammann-freres, home-cooking, water-rowing-surf-swim-fit, creative-practice, entertainment-tastes, spa-wellness-guide, golf-guide, cycling-guide, fishing-guide, dress-code-guide, local-markets-guide, hossegor-local, home-and-spaces, etc.
  - creative-production/: oil-painting, hasselblad-fuji-capture-one, ableton-push-3, blackmagic-design, capture-one-pro, davinci-resolve, blender-claude-mcp.
  - luxury-hotels/: france-palaces, livethelife-places, ski-mountain-resorts, southwest-france-5-star-complete, Cape Town, Lisbon, Brussels, Amsterdam, Monaco, Ibiza, etc.
  - michelin-restaurants/: paris, biarritz-region, bordeaux-region, landes-*, basque-coast, southwest-france-michelin-stars-complete, etc.
  - wine-tasting/: sommelier-playbook, bordeaux-*, burgundy-*, rhone-*, loire-*, champagne, alsace, south-african-wines, whiskey, armagnac, etc.
  - surf/: iconic-pointbreaks-world, surf-spots-by-country, surf-resorts, landes-basque-home-breaks, jbay, etc.

  - Health & fitness and Fitness coach: reference swimming-daily-winter-pools; primary sport 1000m+ daily; winter = wetsuit or Palais/Caudalie when open.
