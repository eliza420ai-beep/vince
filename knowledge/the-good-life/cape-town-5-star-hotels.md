# Cape Town 5-Star Hotels

## The Peninsula

### One&Only Cape Town
- **Location:** V&A Waterfront
- **Vibe:** Island resort in the city
- **Highlights:**
  - Private island setting on Marina Rise
  - Nobu restaurant on-site
  - Spa Island with outdoor treatment rooms
  - Views of Table Mountain
  - Vista Bar for sundowners
- **Why It's Elite:** Best V&A Waterfront location, walking distance to everything
- **Room Count:** 131 rooms and suites
- **Price Range:** $$$$$

### Belmond Mount Nelson Hotel
- **Location:** Gardens, city bowl
- **Vibe:** Pink palace, old-world Cape Town glamour
- **Highlights:**
  - Iconic pink colonial building (1899)
  - 9 acres of manicured gardens
  - Planet Bar for cocktails
  - High tea tradition
  - Central location near museums & Company's Garden
- **Why It's Elite:** Most historic luxury hotel in Cape Town, "The Nellie"
- **Room Count:** 198 rooms
- **Price Range:** $$$$

### Taj Cape Town
- **Location:** City center, St George's Mall
- **Highlights:**
  - Historic building (former Reserve Bank)
  - Jiva Spa
  - Mint restaurant
  - Walking distance to Parliament & Company's Garden
- **Why It's Elite:** Indian hospitality meets Cape heritage
- **Price Range:** $$$$

## Atlantic Seaboard

### Ellerman House
- **Location:** Bantry Bay
- **Vibe:** Private boutique mansion
- **Highlights:**
  - Edwardian villa (1906)
  - Contemporary art collection (100+ South African works)
  - Wine gallery with 7,500+ bottles
  - Oceanfront spa
  - 11 exclusive rooms only
- **Why It's Elite:** Most exclusive art hotel in Africa, ultra-intimate
- **Room Count:** 11 rooms
- **Price Range:** $$$$$+

### The Twelve Apostles Hotel & Spa
- **Location:** Camps Bay, between mountains & ocean
- **Vibe:** Coastal sanctuary
- **Highlights:**
  - Direct Table Mountain National Park views
  - Azure restaurant
  - Outdoor pool on cliff edge
  - Cinema lounge
  - Spa with mountain & ocean views
- **Why It's Elite:** Most dramatic coastal setting
- **Room Count:** 70 rooms
- **Price Range:** $$$$$

### Cape Grace
- **Location:** V&A Waterfront yacht marina
- **Vibe:** Boutique waterfront elegance
- **Highlights:**
  - Private yacht marina location
  - Signal restaurant
  - Whisky bar (550+ whiskies)
  - Bascule Bar
  - Table Mountain views
- **Why It's Elite:** Best boutique on the waterfront
- **Room Count:** 120 rooms
- **Price Range:** $$$$$

### Equus Dolomite
- **Location:** Camps Bay beachfront
- **Highlights:**
  - Directly on Camps Bay beach
  - Suites with ocean views
  - Rooftop bar
  - Walking distance to restaurants
- **Why It's Elite:** Prime beach position
- **Price Range:** $$$$

## Constantia Wine Valley

### The Cellars-Hohenort
- **Location:** Constantia
- **Vibe:** Garden estate in wine country
- **Highlights:**
  - 9 acres of botanical gardens
  - The Conservatory restaurant
  - Liz McGrath art collection
  - Adjacent to Kirstenbosch
  - Cape Dutch architecture
- **Why It's Elite:** Garden luxury in wine country
- **Room Count:** 53 rooms
- **Price Range:** $$$$

### Steenberg Hotel
- **Location:** Constantia wine estate
- **Highlights:**
  - On working wine farm (1682)
  - Championship golf course
  - Catharina's Restaurant (fine dining)
  - Wine tastings
  - Historic Cape Dutch manor
- **Why It's Elite:** Golf, wine, history combo
- **Room Count:** 24 suites
- **Price Range:** $$$$

## Outside Cape Town (Worth the Drive)

### Delaire Graff Estate
- **Location:** Stellenbosch (45 min from Cape Town)
- **Vibe:** Wine estate ultra-luxury
- **Highlights:**
  - Owned by Laurence Graff (diamonds)
  - Two fine dining restaurants
  - Spa sanctuary
  - Contemporary art collection
  - Panoramic vineyard views
  - Estate winery
- **Why It's Elite:** Most luxurious wine estate in Africa
- **Room Count:** 10 lodges
- **Price Range:** $$$$$+

## Quick Selection Guide

**Best Overall:** One&Only Cape Town (location + amenities)
**Most Exclusive:** Ellerman House (only 11 rooms, art collection)
**Best Views:** Twelve Apostles (mountains + ocean)
**Best for History:** Belmond Mount Nelson (pink palace since 1899)
**Best Beach:** The Marly (Camps Bay beachfront)
**Best Wine Country:** Delaire Graff Estate (ultimate luxury)
**Best Gardens:** The Cellars-Hohenort (botanical paradise)
**Best Art:** Ellerman House (museum-quality collection)
