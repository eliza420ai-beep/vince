# Le Relais de la Poste — Magescq (Landes)

**The top restaurant in the Landes.** 2 Michelin stars, Relais & Châteaux. Chef Jean Coussau. Classic, locavore-focused cuisine. ~20 min from Hossegor via D652.

## Why it's on the allowlist

- **2★ Michelin** — special occasion, celebration, "the top local."
- **Lunch Wed–Sun** 12:00–13:30. **Closed Mon–Tue.**
- **€80–150/person** lunch (menu dégustation — let the kitchen guide you). Smart-casual dress.
- **Reservation required** — call 2–3 days ahead; ask for garden terrace in summer.
- Same property as **Côté Quillier** (casual bistro); same phone, specify which when calling.
