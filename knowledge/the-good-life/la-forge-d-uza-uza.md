# La Forge d'Uza — Uza (Landes)

**Simple, quality lakeside lunch at Domaine d'Uza.** Terrace by the lake. Same estate as La Table du Marensin (more upscale). ~25 min from Hossegor. **€25–40/person.** Casual.

## Why it's on the allowlist

- **Lakeside, relaxed** — Fri–Sun lunch 12:00–13:30; Wed–Thu dinner only 19:00–21:00. Closed Mon–Tue.
- **€25–40/person.** Casual dress. Reservation recommended.
- **Use case:** Casual lakeside lunch when you don't want the full gastronomic experience at La Table du Marensin.
