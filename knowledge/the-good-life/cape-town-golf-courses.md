# Cape Town & South Africa Championship Golf Courses

## Cape Town Area

### Steenberg Golf Club
- **Location:** Constantia, Cape Town (20 min from city)
- **Designer:** Peter Matkovich
- **Type:** Parkland, wine estate course
- **Holes:** 18
- **Par:** 72
- **Highlights:**
  - Oldest wine estate in SA (1682)
  - Mountain views, vineyards
  - 5-star hotel on-site
  - Catharina's Restaurant
  - Challenging layout with water features
- **Why Play:** Best course in Cape Town proper, luxury combo
- **Green Fee:** $$$

### Royal Cape Golf Club
- **Location:** Wynberg, Cape Town
- **Founded:** 1885 (oldest golf club in SA)
- **Type:** Parkland
- **Holes:** 18
- **Par:** 72
- **Highlights:**
  - Historic club with dress code
  - Mature trees, classic layout
  - Table Mountain views
  - Private members club (visitors with intro)
- **Why Play:** Heritage & tradition
- **Green Fee:** $$$

### Westlake Golf Club
- **Location:** Muizenberg, Cape Town
- **Type:** Wetland course
- **Holes:** 18
- **Par:** 72
- **Highlights:**
  - Unique wetland setting
  - Birdlife sanctuary
  - False Bay views
  - Challenging water features
- **Why Play:** Unique ecosystem course
- **Green Fee:** $$

### Clovelly Country Club
- **Location:** Clovelly, Cape Town
- **Type:** Parkland
- **Holes:** 18
- **Par:** 72
- **Highlights:**
  - False Bay ocean views
  - Challenging coastal winds
  - Well-maintained fairways
- **Why Play:** Ocean views, members club feel

## Garden Route (2-4 hours from Cape Town)

### Fancourt (George)
**The Links**
- **Location:** George (4.5 hours from Cape Town)
- **Designer:** Gary Player
- **Type:** True links course
- **Holes:** 18
- **Par:** 73
- **Highlights:**
  - Only true links course in SA
  - Hosted Presidents Cup (2003)
  - Scottish-style links design
  - Severe bunkering, dramatic landscape
- **Prestige:** Top 100 World Rankings
- **Why Play:** Best links experience in Africa
- **Green Fee:** $$$$

**Montagu**
- **Designer:** Gary Player
- **Type:** Parkland
- **Par:** 72
- **Highlights:**
  - More forgiving than The Links
  - Beautiful tree-lined fairways
  - Outeniqua Mountain views
- **Why Play:** Scenic parkland alternative

**Outeniqua**
- **Type:** Resort course
- **Par:** 72
- **Why Play:** Easier option, good for all levels

**Fancourt Hotel & Spa**
- 5-star resort on property
- Multiple restaurants
- Luxury lodges
- Complete golf vacation package

### Pinnacle Point (Mossel Bay)
- **Location:** Mossel Bay (4 hours from Cape Town)
- **Designer:** Peter Matkovich
- **Type:** Cliff-top links
- **Holes:** 18
- **Par:** 72
- **Highlights:**
  - Dramatic cliff-top location over Indian Ocean
  - 7 holes on cliff edge (90m above sea)
  - Whale watching from course (Jun-Nov)
  - Links-style with ocean views
- **Prestige:** Top 100 World Rankings
- **Lodging:** Pinnacle Point Beach & Golf Resort
- **Why Play:** Most dramatic ocean course in world
- **Green Fee:** $$$$

## Practical Info

**Best Season:** Oct-Apr (summer) - warm, long days
**Shoulder:** Mar-May, Sep-Oct (best weather, fewer crowds)
**Winter:** Jun-Aug (cooler, can be windy)

**Green Fees:**
- $$ = $50-80 USD
- $$$ = $80-150 USD
- $$$$ = $150-250 USD

**Tips:**
- Book tee times in advance (especially Fancourt, Pinnacle Point)
- Hire caddies where available
- Wind can be challenging (esp. coastal courses)
- Private clubs need member introduction
- Most top courses have pro shops, club rental

**Caddies:** Expected at premium courses, ~$20-30 tip USD
