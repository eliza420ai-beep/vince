# Dress Code Guide — When to Wear What

Quick reference for dressing appropriately. Use when user asks for **what to wear**, **dress code**, **formal**, **casual**, **smart casual**, or **outfit**.

---

## The Dress Code Spectrum

| Code | What It Means | When |
|------|---------------|------|
| **Casual** | Clean, neat, no holes | Beach, home, market |
| **Smart Casual** | Polished but relaxed | Dinner at casual restaurant, day out |
| **Business** | Blazer, dress pants | Meetings, professional events |
| **Formal** | Suit, tie, dress | Weddings, ceremonies |
| **Black Tie** | Tuxedo, floor-length | Galas, very formal events |

---

## Occasion Guides

### Restaurant Dress Codes

| Restaurant Type | Expected Dress | Examples |
|----------------|----------------|----------|
| **Casual bistro** | Nice jeans, clean shirt | Auberge du Lavoir, local |
| **Nice restaurant** | Smart casual | Most in Biarritz |
| **Fine dining (1-2★)** | Smart casual to business | Arzak, Mugaritz |
| **3★ Michelin** | Business formal | Arzak, Les Prés d'Eugénie |

### Beach / Pool

- **Beach**: Swimsuit, cover-up, flip flops
- **Pool lunch**: Nice swimsuit, espadrilles, linen shirt
- **Hotel pool**: Swimsuit, flip flops, cover-up

### Golf

- **Course**: Golf attire (collared shirt, tailored shorts/pants)
- **Clubhouse**: Smart casual
- **Pro tip**: No denim, no t-shirts

### Ski

- **On slope**: Technical layers, ski jacket, helmet (recommended)
- **Lunch**: Smart casual, ski boots OK at mountain restaurants

### Night Out

| Type | Dress |
|------|-------|
| **Pintxos crawl** | Smart casual |
| **Nice dinner** | Business (jacket) |
| **Club** | Smart casual to business |
| **Special occasion** | Business formal |

## Destination Dress Codes

### San Sebastián

- **Pintxos**: Smart casual (no shorts at night)
- **Fine dining**: Jacket recommended at Arzak
- **Beach**: Swimwear only

### Bordeaux / Wine

- **Château visits**: Smart casual
- **Fine dining**: Business

### Paris

- **General**: More dressed up than south
- **Dinner**: Always jacket for nice restaurants
- **Day**: Polished, not flashy

### Ski

- **Resort**: Technical but stylish
- **Dinner**: Smart casual (no ski boots)

## The "Always" Rules

- **Fit matters more than price** — Tailored clothes beat expensive baggy ones
- **Clean** — No stains, holes, wrinkles
- **Match** — Belt matches shoes
- **Less is more** — Don't over-accessorize
- **When in doubt** — Dress up slightly more than needed

## Kelly's Style Advice

1. **Fit is everything** — Get things tailored
2. **Quality over quantity** — Fewer, better pieces
3. **White sneakers** — The most versatile shoe
4. **Blazer fixes everything** — Throw over any outfit
5. **Don't try too hard** — Effortless > try-hard
