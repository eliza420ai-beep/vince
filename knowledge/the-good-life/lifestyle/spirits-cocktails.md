## Classic Cocktails

### The Essentials

**Old Fashioned**
- 60ml bourbon or rye
- 1 sugar cube or 5ml syrup
- 2-3 dashes Angostura
- Orange peel
- *Method*: Muddle sugar, add spirit, stir, ice, express orange

**Martini**
- 60ml gin
- 10ml dry vermouth
- *Method*: Stir, strain, lemon twist or olive

**Margarita**
- 50ml tequila reposado
- 25ml lime juice
- 15ml agave syrup or Cointreau
- *Method*: Shake, strain, salted rim

**Daiquiri**
- 50ml white rum
- 25ml lime juice
- 15ml simple syrup
- *Method*: Shake, strain

**Negroni**
- 30ml gin
- 30ml Campari
- 30ml sweet vermouth
- *Method*: Stir, rocks, orange

**Whiskey Sour**
- 50ml bourbon
- 25ml lemon juice
- 15ml simple syrup
- 1 egg white (optional)
- *Method*: Shake, strain

## French Cocktails

**French 75**
- 30ml gin
- 15ml lemon
- 10ml sugar
- Champagne top
- *Method*: Shake, top with Champagne

**French Connection**
- 35ml Cognac
- 35ml Amaretto
- *Method*: Stir, rocks

**Sidecar**
- 50ml Cognac
- 20ml Cointreau
- 20ml lemon
- *Method*: Shake, sugar rim

**Between the Sheets**
- 20ml Cognac
- 20ml white rum
- 20ml Cointreau
- 20ml lemon
- *Method*: Shake, strain

## Ice

**The secret**:
- Use large cubes (slower melt)
- Use crushed ice for shaking
- Store in freezer (no freezer taste)
- **King's Ice** — available at supermarkets

## Kelly's Bar Advice

1. **Quality over quantity** — Better spirits, fewer bottles
2. **Start with gin + whiskey** — Most versatile
3. **Add armagnac** — Support local
4. **Make your own syrups** — Simple syrup, honey syrup
5. **Ice matters** — Big cubes, good tonic
