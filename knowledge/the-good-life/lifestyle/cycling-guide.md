# Cycling Guide — Road Cycling in Southwest France

Complete guide for road cycling in the region. Use when user asks for **cycling**, **road bike**, **bike ride**, **cycling routes**, or **where to ride**.

---

## Best Routes

### Coastal Routes

**Biarritz → Hendaye (The Classic)**
- **Distance**: 40 km round trip
- **Elevation**: 200m
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Route**: Coastal road (D810), minimal traffic
- **Highlights**: Ocean views, beach stops

**Hossegor → Capbreton Loop**
- **Distance**: 25 km
- **Elevation**: 150m
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Route**: Lake loop, pine forest

**Arcachon → Pyla-sur-Mer**
- **Distance**: 30 km
- **Elevation**: 100m
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Route**: D218, Dune du Pilat access

### Hill Routes

**Basque Interior Climb**
- **Distance**: 60 km
- **Elevation**: 800m
- **Difficulty**: Medium-Hard
- **Route**: Bidarray climb, quintessential Basque
- **Best climb**: Puerto de Izpegi

**Saint-Émilion Loop**
- **Distance**: 45 km
- **Elevation**: 300m
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Route**: Vineyard roads, Dordogne
- **Highlights**: Wine stops, Château views

### Long Routes

**Bordeaux → Arcachon**
- **Distance**: 120 km
- **Elevation**: 400m
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Best for**: Bucket list, forest + ocean

## Weather & Conditions

### Best Months

| Month | Conditions | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| **May** | Ideal | Warm, low wind |
| **June** | Good | Summer start |
| **September** | Excellent | Best of year |
| **October** | Good | Cool, autumn colors |

### Wind Patterns

- **Morning**: Offshore (favorable)
- **Afternoon**: Onshore (headwind)
- **Tip**: Ride early, return afternoon

### Rain

- **Winter**: Wet roads, fewer cyclists
- **Summer**: Afternoon thunderstorms possible
- **Year-round**: Bring rain jacket

---

## Cycling Etiquette

- **Ride single file** on busy roads
- **Use hand signals** for turns, obstacles
- **Thank drivers** who give space
- **Stop at red lights** — French police enforce
- **Wear helmet** — Recommended, required for children
