## Day Trip Checklist

### Before You Go

- [ ] Check snow conditions (snow-forecast.com)
- [ ] Book rental equipment (or bring boots)
- [ ] Lift passes: Buy online to skip queue
- [ ] Gas up (full tank recommended)
- [ ] Pack: Layers, gloves, goggles, sunscreen

### Timing

- **Leave**: 6-7am (arrive by 8:30 for parking)
- **Ski**: 9am-4pm
- **Return**: 5pm departure, home by 7pm

### What to Bring

- **Ski jacket** (waterproof)
- **Layers** (base + mid + shell)
- **Gloves** (waterproof)
- **Goggles** (UV essential)
- **Sunscreen** (snow reflection = burn)
- **Lip balm** (cold wind)
- **Snacks** (or buy at resort)
- **Cash** (for lunch, parking)

## Driving Tips

### To Spain (Candanchú, Formigal)

- **Border**: Irun/Dondis — usually no delay
- **Toll**: ~€15 each way (AP-8, A-136)
- **Parking**: At resort (€10-15/day)
- **Time**: 1h 30min to Formigal

### To France (Gourette, Cauterets)

- **Route**: A64 to Pau, then D937
- **Mountain roads**: Can be snowy — chains required
- **Time**: 1h 15min to Gourette

**Rent at resort** (easier):

**Bring if**:
- Have good boots already
- Going multiple times
- Have roof rack
