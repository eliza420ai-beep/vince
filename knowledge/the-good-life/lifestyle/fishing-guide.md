# Fishing Guide — Southwest France & Atlantic

Complete guide for fishing in the region. Use when user asks for **fishing**, **fish**, **deep sea**, **surf fishing**, **spearfishing**, or **catch**.

---

## Types of Fishing

### Shore Fishing (Surf Casting)

**Where**:
- Hossegor beach
- Capbreton
- Biarritz (Côte des Basques)
- Anglet

**Best for**: Bass, mullet, sole

**Gear**:
- 12-13ft surf rod
- Fixed spool reel (4000-6000)
- 30-50g leads
- Hooks sizes 2-6

**Techniques**:
- Lure fishing at dawn/dusk
- Bait fishing (mackerel, squid)
- Night fishing for bass

---

### Deep Sea Fishing (Boat)

**Where**: Biarritz, Capbreton, Arcachon

**Charters**:
- Biarritz: +33 6 12 34 56 78
- Capbreton: +33 6 98 76 54 32

**What to catch**:
- **Bass** (bar): Year-round, best spring-fall
- **Sea bream** (dorade): Summer
- **Mackerel** (maquerau): Spring-fall
- **Bonito** (pélamide): Summer
- **Tuna** (thon): Late summer

**Cost**: ~€200-400/day for charter

---

### Lake Fishing

**Where**:
- Lake Hossegor (Etang de Hardy)
- Lake Uza
- Lac de Moliets
- Lac d'Hossegor

**Best for**:
- Carp
- Pike
- Perch
- Catfish

**Tips**:
- Early morning best
- Use bait (corn, boilies)
- Check local regulations

---

### Spearfishing

**Where**:
- Biarritz (Côte des Basques)
- Anglet (La Barre)
- Hossegor (north beach)
- Côte Vermeille (south)

**Best season**: July-September (calm seas, warm water)

**Gear**:
- Mask, snorkel, fins
- Speargun (polespear or rubber)
- Wetsuit (3mm summer, 5mm winter)

**Species**:
- Sea bream (dorade)
- Sea bass (bar)
- Octopus
- Conger eel

**Rules**:
- License required (permis de pêche)
- Minimum sizes apply
- No diving within 100m of fishermen

## Bait & Lures

### Natural Bait

- **Mackerel**: For bass, best when fresh
- **Squid**: All species
- **Sardines**: Chum, bait
- **Shrimp**: For bream, sole

### Lures

- **Bass**: Surface lures (poppers), soft plastics
- **Mackerel**: Metal jigs, feathers
- **General**: Spoons, plugs

---

## Regulations

### Licenses

- **Shore fishing**: License required (carte de pêche)
- **Boat fishing**: License included with charter
- **Spearfishing**: Specific license + medical certificate

### Minimum Sizes (Atlantic)

| Species | Minimum |
|---------|---------|
| Bass | 36 cm |
| Sea bream | 23 cm |
| Sole | 24 cm |
| Mackerel | 20 cm |

### Closed Seasons

- Some species have quotas
- Check latest regulations

## Quick Tips

1. **Dawn + dusk** — Best times for bass
2. **Check conditions** — Wind matters
3. **License first** — Always
4. **Respect limits** — Take only what you need
5. **Clean up** — Leave no trace

## Fishing + Food

**Best catch → best preparation**:

- **Bass**: Grill whole, pan-fried fillets
- **Sea bream**: Whole roasted
- **Sole**: Meunière
- **Mackerel**: Grilled with lemon
- **Octopus**: Grilled, aioli

**Best restaurant to cook your catch**:
- Le Chalet (Biarritz)
- Chez Mattin (Hossegor)
