# Spa & Wellness Guide — Southwest France

Complete guide to thermal spas, thalassotherapy centers, and wellness retreats in the region. Use when user asks for **spa day**, **thalasso**, **thermal spa**, **wellness retreat**, or **pool day**.

---

## Our Top Palaces for Wellness

### 1. Les Sources de Caudalie ⭐⭐ (PALACE)

**Vinotherapy Pioneer**

- **Location**: Martillac (30 min from Bordeaux)
- **Spa**: Vinothérapie Spa — pioneered grape-based treatments
- **Treatments**: Crushed Cabernet scrub, Merlot bath, grape seed massage
- **Pool**: Heated outdoor + indoor
- **Best for**: Wine lovers who want spa, vineyard immersion
- **Open**: February 5, 2026
- **Price**: ~200-400€ for treatments
- **Book**: +33 5 57 83 83 83

---

### 2. Hôtel du Palais ⭐⭐ (PALACE)

**Imperial Thalasso**

- **Location**: Biarritz (on Grande Plage)

- **Treatments**: Seawater treatments, ocean-view massages
- **Pool**: Heated outdoor + indoor
- **Best for**: Ocean + grandeur, surf + spa combo
- **Open**: February 12, 2026
- **Book**: +33 5 59 41 64 00

### 3. Les Prés d'Eugénie ⭐⭐⭐ (PALACE)

## Spa Day Checklist

**Before you go:**
- [ ] Book treatment in advance (most require 48h notice)
- [ ] Confirm opening hours (often closed Mon-Tue)
- [ ] Arrive 30 min early for relaxation area
- [ ] Bring: swimsuit, flip flops, book (for relaxation time)

**At the spa:**
- [ ] Start with thermal circuit (sauna → steam → cold → repeat)
- [ ] Book massage for middle of session
- [ ] End with relaxation area and tea
- [ ] Stay 3-4 hours minimum

## Booking Tips

- **Book direct** for best availability
- **Weekdays** (especially Wed-Thu) = easier bookings
- **Off-season** = more availability, better rates
- **Day packages** often better value than single treatments
- **Verify closures** — many closed January-February

---

**For hotel-only spa access:** Some hotels sell day passes to their spa facilities even if not staying overnight. Call to ask.
