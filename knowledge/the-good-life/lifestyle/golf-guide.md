## Golf Tips for SW France

### Best Time to Play

- **Spring (Apr-May)**: Perfect temps, fewer crowds
- **Fall (Sep-Oct)**: Best conditions, harvest season
- **Summer**: Early morning tee times (8am), hot midday
- **Winter**: Limited daylight, courses can be wet

### Booking

- Book 48h+ ahead for weekends
- Early morning (8-9am) best for conditions
- Municipal courses: book online
- Private courses: book via hotel concierge

### What to Bring

- Golf clubs (or rent at course)
- Wet weather gear (Landes rain is real)
- Sun protection (cap, sunscreen)
- Pitch marker (coin)
