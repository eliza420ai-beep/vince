## Shooting Modes

### When to Use What

| Mode | When | Settings |
|------|------|----------|
| **Aperture Priority (A/Av)** | Most situations | Set f-stop, camera picks shutter |
| **Manual (M)** | Studio, controlled | Full control |
| **Shutter Priority (S/Tv)** | Action, motion | Set shutter, camera picks f-stop |

### Exposure Triangle

| Element | Effect | Use |
|---------|--------|-----|
| **Aperture (f/)** | Depth of field | f/2.5 = shallow, f/8 = sharp |
| **Shutter** | Motion blur | 1/500 = frozen, 1/30 = blur |
| **ISO** | Noise | 64-400 = clean, 800+ = grain |

## Lighting

### Natural Light

**Golden Hour** (Best)
- Morning: First hour after sunrise
- Evening: Last hour before sunset
- Soft, warm, long shadows

**Blue Hour**
- 20 min before sunrise / after sunset
- Cool, moody, city lights

**Harsh Midday**
- Use shade
- Look for open shade
- Indoor window light

### Working with Light

- **Side light**: Dramatic, textured
- **Back light**: Rim light, silhouettes
- **Front light**: Flat, even
- **Overcast**: Soft, even, forgiving

## Composition

### The Essentials

**Rule of Thirds**
- Divide frame into 9 squares
- Place subjects on lines or intersections
- More dynamic than centered

**Leading Lines**
- Roads, fences, shorelines
- Guide eye through frame
- Create depth

**Framing**
- Use windows, doors, arches
- Natural frames add depth
- Draw attention to subject

**Negative Space**
- Empty space around subject
- Creates breathing room
- Adds drama

### The 5 Composition Rules

1. **Rule of Thirds** — Place on lines
2. **Leading Lines** — Guide the eye
3. **Framing** — Use natural frames
4. **Symmetry** — Mirror images
5. **Patterns** — Repeating elements

## Subjects

### Travel Photography

**Landscapes**
- Use tripod
- Small aperture (f/8-f/11)
- Focus at hyperfocal distance
- Bracket for HDR

**Street**
- 35-50mm equivalent
- Be ready, anticipate moments
- Shoot from hip or discreet
- Ask permission when appropriate

**Portraits**
- Wide aperture (f/2.5-f/4)
- Focus on eyes
- Use reflectors or fill flash
- Shoot in shade or golden hour

**Food**
- Natural light best
- 45° or overhead angle
- Minimalist styling
- Use Macro or close-up lens

## Maintenance

### Camera Care

- **Sensor cleaning**: Weekly or as needed
- **Lens cleaning**: Before every shoot
- **Body**: Use rocket blower
- **Storage**: Dry cabinet for humidity

### Storage

- **Cards**: Always format in camera
- **Backup**: 2 copies minimum (SSD + cloud)

## Tips

1. **Shoot in RAW** — Maximum flexibility
2. **Expose to the right** — Don't clip highlights
3. **Use a tripod** — Sharpness matters
4. **Back up everything** — At least 2 copies
5. **Edit less** — Get it right in camera
