# Local Markets Guide — Southwest France

Complete guide to markets in the region. Use when user asks for **market**, **where to buy**, **groceries**, **farmers market**, or **provisions**.

---

## Hossegor Area

### Saturday Morning Market — Main Event

**Hossegor Market**
- **When**: Saturday morning (typically 8am-1pm)
- **Location**: Centre ville, near the church
- **What to buy**:
  - **Poulet fermier des Landes** — Yellow-skinned free-range chicken
  - **Duck products** — Magret, confit legs, foie gras
  - **Cheese** — Ossau-Iraty (Basque sheep cheese)
  - **Vegetables** — Local, seasonal
  - **Honey** — Landes honey
  - **Madiran wine** — Local tannat

**Tip**: Arrive early (8-9am) for best selection. Vendors start packing up by 12:30.

## Dax

### Wednesday & Saturday Market

**Marché de Dax**
- **When**: Wednesday morning, Saturday morning
- **Location**: Centre ville
- **What to buy**:
  - Larger selection than Hossegor
  - Poultry, duck, goose
  - Local products
  - Cheeses
  - Olive oil

**Tip**: Worth the drive for weekly provisioning.

## Market Tips

### Timing

- **Arrive early**: 8-9am for best selection
- **Mid-morning**: Can still find good items, vendors more flexible on price
- **Before close**: Some vendors discount remaining items

### Payment

- **Cash preferred** — Many vendors don't take cards
- **Bring small bills** — Easier for vendors

### Etiquette

- **Don't touch** — Let the vendor select
- **Ask for samples** — Cheese, charcuterie vendors often offer
- **Compare** — Walk the full market before buying

### Bags

- **Bring your own** — Canvas tote or reusable bag
- **Cooler bag** — For meat, fish in summer
