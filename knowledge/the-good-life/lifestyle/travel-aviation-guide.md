# Travel & Aviation Guide

Complete guide for private aviation, commercial first class, and airport logistics. Use when user asks about **flights**, **private jet**, **airport**, **terminal**, **lounge**, or **how to get to [destination]**.

## Private Aviation

### From Bordeaux (BMRK)

**Closest private airport**: Bordeaux-Mérignac (BMRK)

- **Distance from Hossegor**: ~1h 15min
- **Runway**: 3,000m
- **FBO Services**: Skyjet, Air Bordeaux, Handling Bordeaux
- **Fuel**: Available
- **Parking**: Limited — book in advance

**From Bordeaux to SW France destinations:**
- Biarritz: 45min flight
- Pau: 40min flight
- Toulouse: 30min flight

### From Biarritz (BIQ)

**Closest**: Biarritz Pays Basque Airport (BIQ)

- **Distance from Hossegor**: ~30min
- **Runway**: 2,200m
- **FBO**: Aeroways, Biarritz Handling
- **Best for**: Direct Biarritz access

**International connections:**
- Paris Le Bourget: 1h 15min
- Nice: 1h
- Geneva: 1h
- London Farnborough: 1h 15min

## Commercial First Class

### Best Routes from Bordeaux (BMRK)

**To Paris:**
- Air France (1h 15min) — First class available
- CDG or ORLY connection

**To Nice:**
- Air France via CDG (2h 30min)

**To Geneva:**
- EasyJet (seasonal)
- Swiss via ZRH

### Best Routes from Biarritz (BIQ)

**To Paris:**
- Air France (1h 30min)
- Orly direct (seasonal)

**To London:**
- EasyJet (seasonal, summer)

### First Class Tips

- **Air France**: Book 14+ days ahead for best availability
- **Lounge Access**: Priority Pass, Amex Platinum, or business class ticket
- **Fast Track**: Bordeaux has priority security

## Booking Tips

- **Private jet**: Book 48h+ in advance for best pricing
- **Helicopter**: Same-day possible, weather permitting
- **Drivers**: Better value for <3h trips
- **First class**: 14 days ahead for availability
- **Lounges**: Priority Pass accepted at most French airports
