## Overview

Belgium's luxury hotel scene rivals larger countries. Brussels has world-class palace hotels, while Bruges, Antwerp, and Knokke offer boutique elegance. The country punches above its weight for luxury.

**Key Areas**:
- **Brussels**: Grand hotels, palace properties
- **Bruges**: Romantic, historic
- **Antwerp**: Design, modern luxury
- **Knokke-Heist**: Coastal, art scene
