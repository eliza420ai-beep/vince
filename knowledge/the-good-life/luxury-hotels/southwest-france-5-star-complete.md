## 1-Night Stay Optimization

**Our standard approach** (we rarely stay over):

1. **Arrive 13:00** — Lunch at hotel restaurant
2. **Afternoon** — Spa treatment or local exploration
3. **Evening** — Pre-dinner drinks, tasting menu dinner
4. **Overnight** — Full breakfast (never skip)
5. **Late checkout** — 12:00

**Best day**: Wednesday (midweek rates, less busy)
