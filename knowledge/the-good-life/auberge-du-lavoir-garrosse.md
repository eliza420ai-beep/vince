# Auberge du Lavoir — Garrosse (Landes)

**Traditional French family auberge.** The **only reliable lunch option most Tuesdays** in the Landes allowlist — Tue, Wed, Thu, Sun 12:00–13:30. ~15 min from Hossegor via D33.

## Why it's on the allowlist

- **Tuesday go-to** — when almost everything else is closed (Mon–Tue), Lavoir is open.
- **Traditional** — family-run, convivial. **Order:** confit de canard. **€20–30/person.** Casual.
- **Walk-in usually OK**; call morning-of to confirm open if unsure.
- **Closed Fri–Sat.** Open Tue, Wed, Thu, Sun 12:00–13:30.
