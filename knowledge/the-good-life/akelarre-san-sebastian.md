# Akelarre — San Sebastián

**3★ Michelin. Chef Pedro Subijana. Pioneer of nueva cocina vasca.** Gulf views. Hotel + **Espazio Oteiza** terrace for lighter bites. **€150–250/person.** Reservation required — 2–3 weeks ahead. Smart-casual. Padre Orcolaga, 56. Tel: +34 943 311 208. Source: The Good Life (thegoodlife.fr). akelarre.net
