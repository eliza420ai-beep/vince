# La Table du Marensin — Uza (Landes)

**Gastronomic restaurant at Domaine d'Uza.** Innovative, regional products, beautiful setting. More upscale than La Forge d'Uza (same estate). ~25 min from Hossegor via D652.

## Why it's on the allowlist

- **Gastronomic** — special occasion lunch or dinner, seasonal tasting menu.
- **Lunch** Wed, Fri–Sun 12:00–13:30; **dinner** Thu–Sat 19:00–21:00. **Closed Mon–Tue.** Thursday is dinner only (no lunch).
- **€40–65/person** lunch. Smart-casual. **Order:** seasonal tasting menu.
- **Reservation required** — call 2–3 days ahead; mention any dietary needs.
