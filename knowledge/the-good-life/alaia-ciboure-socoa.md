# Alaïa — Ciboure/Socoa

**Only restaurant directly on Socoa beach.** Chef Fabrice Naacke (ex-Paris). Ultra-local: thon de Saint-Jean, turbot espagnol, chipirons only in season (September). Bio wines from across the border. **€40–65/person.** Check schedule. Reservation required — 2–3 days ahead. Smart-casual. Plage de Socoa. Phone: +33 5 59 47 43 79. Source: The Good Life (thegoodlife.fr). restaurantalaia.com
