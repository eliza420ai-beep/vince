# Côté Quillier — Magescq (Landes)

**Bistro at Le Relais de la Poste.** Same address and phone as the 2★; relaxed version of the starred experience. 1 Gault&Millau toque. Market-driven Landaise cuisine, €39 set menu. ~20 min from Hossegor via D652.

## Why it's on the allowlist

- **Casual option** at the Relais — fireplace or terrace, no need for full formal.
- **Lunch Wed–Sun** 12:00–13:45. **Closed Mon–Tue.**
- **€30–50/person.** **Order:** Landaise salad, then whatever's on the ardoise. Casual dress.
- **Reservation** — call day before; **same number as Relais** (+33 5 58 47 70 25), specify Quillier.
