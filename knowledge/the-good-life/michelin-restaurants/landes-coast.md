# Landes Coast Fine Dining

> **Region**: Hossegor, Seignosse, Pyla-sur-Mer
> **Vibe**: Surf culture meets gastronomy, ocean views, pine forests

---

## Villa de l'Etang Blanc (Seignosse)

**1-Star Michelin | Intimate lakeside retreat**

- **Address**: D432, 40510 Seignosse
- **Website**: https://www.villaetangblanc.fr/
- **Phone**: +33 5 58 72 80 15
- **Hotel**: 3\* charming, 6 rooms
- **Chef**: David Sulpice (1 star since 2019)

**Why We Love It**:

- Ultra-local sourcing (morning market runs)
- Lakeside setting in nature reserve
- Family-run (Magali + David Sulpice)
- 10 min to Seignosse beaches
- Champagne bar by the lake (summer)

**Best For**: Romantic escape, nature lovers, understated elegance

---

## Les Hortensias du Lac (Hossegor)

**Fontenille Collection | Art Deco surf lodge**

- **Address**: 1578 Av. du Tour du Lac, 40150 Soorts-Hossegor
- **Website**: https://www.leshortensiasdulac.fontenille-collection.com/
- **Phone**: +33 5 58 43 99 00
- **Style**: Art Deco mansion, sustainable luxury

**Dining**:

- Chef Philippe Moreno (ex-Gérald Passedat)
- Marine-focused cuisine with Southwest terroir
- Terrace with exceptional lake view

**Note**: Closed for winter, reopening March 2026

**Best For**: Lake views, surf lifestyle, Clef Verte eco-certification

## 70 Hectares... & l'Océan (Seignosse)

**Fontenille Collection | Golf & ocean retreat**

- **Address**: 901 Av. du Belvédère, 40510 Seignosse
- **Website**: https://www.70hectares.fontenille-collection.com/
- **Phone**: +33 5 58 45 76 16
- **Style**: Contemporary forest lodge, 32 rooms

**Restaurant SEI**:

- Japanese-Landais fusion
- Izakaya sharing plates
- Brunch on Sundays (iconic)
- Matcha cocktails, Nikka whisky

**Best For**: Golf weekends, surf trips, Japanese fusion lovers

**Note**: Closed for winter, reopening March 2026

## La Co(o)rniche (Pyla-sur-Mer)

**5-Star | Philippe Starck design | Dune du Pyla**

- **Address**: Dune du Pyla, 33115 Pyla-sur-Mer
- **Website**: https://lacoorniche.com/
- **Phone**: Contact via website
- **Style**: Neo-Basque architecture, 29 rooms + cabins

**Experience**:

- Unparalleled views of Banc d'Arguin
- Seafood platters, tapas bar
- DJ until 2am (very lively bar scene)
- Spa at sister property Ha(a)ïtza

**Warning**: Very noisy at night due to festive bar. Book rooms carefully.

**Closure**: January 5 - February 4, 2026

**Best For**: Sunset drinks, seafood platters, Instagram-worthy views

## Ha(a)ïtza (Pyla-sur-Mer)

**5-Star | Philippe Starck | 2-Star Skiff Club**

- **Address**: Boulevard de l'Océan, 33115 Pyla-sur-Mer
- **Website**: https://haaitza.com/
- **Phone**: Contact via website
- **Style**: Neo-Basque mansion, 38 rooms + apartment

**Restaurants**:

- **Skiff Club** (2\* Michelin + 1 green star) - Chef Stéphane Carrade
- **Brasserie Ha(a)ïtza** - all-day dining, continuous service
- **Café Ha(a)ïtza** - bistro across the street
- **Bar Ha(a)ïtza** - cocktails 9h-1h

**Extras**:

- Spa CODAGE
- David Lucas salon
- La Pâtisserie de Famille
- Heated pool with glass roof

**Closure**: Skiff Club closed until February 5, 2026

**Best For**: Gastronomy pilgrimage, sophisticated beach holiday
