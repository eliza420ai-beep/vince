## Domaine d'Uza (Lakeside Options)

### La Forge d'Uza

**36 Rue du Lac, 40170 Uza, France**

- Style: Simple, quality local cuisine
- Vibe: Terrace by the lake at Domaine d'Uza
- Notes: Part of the larger Domaine d'Uza property
- Open: Wed-Thu dinner 19:00-21:00; Fri-Sat lunch 12:00-13:30 + dinner 19:00-21:00; Sun lunch 12:00-13:30. Closed Mon-Tue. Phone 09 70 22 90 21.
- Use case: Casual lakeside lunch

### La Table du Marensin

**115 Rue des Castets, 40170 Uza, France**

- Style: Gastronomic/modern local cuisine
- Vibe: Innovative with regional products, beautiful setting
- Notes: Also at Domaine d'Uza, more upscale than La Forge
- Open: **Wed, Fri-Sun 12:00-13:30** lunch; Thu dinner only 19:00-21:00. Closed Mon-Tue.
- Phone: 09 70 22 90 21
- Use case: Special occasion lunch (Wed, Fri-Sun)

## Special Occasion (Michelin)

### Le Relais de la Poste (Main Restaurant)

**24 Avenue de Maremne, 40140 Magescq, France**

- Style: 2-Michelin-star classic cuisine
- Vibe: Upscale, locavore-focused
- Notes: Chef Jean Coussau, Relais & Châteaux property
- Open: **Wed-Sun 12:00-13:30** (CLOSED Mon-Tue)
- Phone: 05 58 47 70 25
- Use case: Very special occasion, celebration

### Côté Quillier (Bistro at Relais de la Poste)

**24 Avenue de Maremne, 40140 Magescq, France**

- Style: Casual bistro option at the same hotel (1 Gault&Millau toque)
- Vibe: Relaxed version of the starred experience, fireplace or terrace
- Notes: Same location, market-driven Landaise cuisine, €39 set menu
- Open: **Wed-Sun 12:00-13:45** (CLOSED Mon-Tue)
- Phone: 05 58 47 70 25
- Use case: When you want the Relais vibe without full formal dining

## Decision Guide by Day

### TUESDAY (Dining Day)

**Go-to**: Auberge du Lavoir (only confirmed local open)
**Backup**: Check Villa Pomade (call 05 58 47 25 76), La Forge (Fri–Sun lunch), or Green Fourchette — hours TBD; call before visiting.

### THURSDAY (Dining Day)

**Options**:

- Maison Devaux (refined, Michelin Bib Gourmand)
- Auberge du Lavoir (family-friendly, traditional)
- Le Relais de la Poste (2-star, special occasion)
- Côté Quillier (casual Relais bistro)

### WEEKEND (Fri-Sun)

All restaurants open - full choice including La Table du Marensin
