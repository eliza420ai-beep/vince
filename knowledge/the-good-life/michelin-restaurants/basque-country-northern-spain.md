# Basque Country / Northern Spain Fine Dining

> **Region**: Basque Country, Northern Spain
> **Vibe**: Core gastronomy focus, world-class Michelin dining, Basque culinary heritage
> **Distance Reference**: 70-180 km from Southwest France base

---

## Arzak

**3-Star Michelin | Juan Mari & Elena Arzak | San Sebastian Legend**

- **Address**: Alcalde J. Elósegui Hiribidea 273, 20015 Donostia-San Sebastián
- **Distance**: 108 km
- **Chef**: Elena Arzak (3rd generation, 3 stars since 1989)
- **Stars**: 3 Michelin Stars

**The Legend**:

- One of Spain's most iconic restaurants
- Founded 1897, 3 stars since 1989
- Elena Arzak continues family legacy with innovative Basque cuisine
- Modernist approach to traditional Basque ingredients

**Best For**: Ultimate gastronomy pilgrimage, once-in-a-lifetime experience, culinary history

---

## Ibai by Paulo Airaudo

**Fine Dining | Paulo Airaudo | San Sebastian**

- **Address**: Getaria Kalea 15, 20005 Donostia-San Sebastián

**Details**:
