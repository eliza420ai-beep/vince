# Petit Grill Basque — Saint-Jean-de-Luz

**Chef Iñaki Aizpitarte (ex-Chateaubriand, Le Dauphin Paris).** Historic Luz institution renovated; original hand-cranked ceiling fans, Louis Floutier frescoes. Tripes de morue, veau rosé beurre pimenté, riz aux carabineros. Bilingual Basque-French menu. **€40–65/person.** Reservation required — 2–3 days ahead. Smart-casual. 4 Rue Saint-Jacques. Source: The Good Life (thegoodlife.fr).

## Why it's on the allowlist

- **Aizpitarte** — godfather of bistronomie; most exciting table in the region per TGL.
- **€40–65/person.** **Reservation required** — 2–3 days ahead. Small room — walk-in risky. Smart-casual.
- **Use case:** Basque fine dining in France; pair with Irouléguy nature.
