# Maison Devaux — Rion-des-Landes (Landes)

**One of the best restaurants in the Landes.** Not the Champagne house: this is the **restaurant and small hotel in Rion-des-Landes**, ~30 min from Hossegor via D824. Michelin **Bib Gourmand** — refined locavore bistrot, modern Landaise cooking.

## Why it's on the allowlist

- **Bib Gourmand** (value, quality, identity). Locavore, seasonal, modern bistrot.
- **Lunch Wed–Sun** 12:30–14:00; **Sunday extended** to 15:00 — take your time.
- **Closed Mon–Tue.** Small dining room; call **day before** to reserve (fills fast).
- **€25–40/person** lunch. Casual dress. **Order:** Cochon noir de Gascogne when on the menu.
- **Rooms upstairs** (€90–130/night) — simple, charming; walk-in often OK. Lunch spot with rooms, so it appears in both Hotels and Restaurants on the allowlist.
