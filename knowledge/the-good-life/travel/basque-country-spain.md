# Basque Country — Spain Day Trip Guide

Complete guide for San Sebastián, Bilbao, and the Spanish Basque coast. Use when user asks for **San Sebastián**, **Bilbao**, **Basque country Spain**, **day trip to Spain**, **pintxos trip**, or **cross border**.

---

## San Sebastián (Donostia)

### The Drive

- **From Hossegor**: 30 min / 45 km
- **Route**: A63 → A8 (E70) → San Sebastián
- **Border**: No border delays usually
- **Parking**: La Concha underground, Old Town

### Best for

- **Pintxos** — the best in the world
- **La Concha beach** — iconic crescent bay
- **Old Town** —Parte Vieja, bars
- **Museums** — San Telmo, Chillida
- **Shopping** — Local designers

### Top Pintxo Bars (Never Skip)

| Bar | Specialty | Location |
|-----|-----------|----------|
| **Bar Nestor** | Tortilla, chorizo | Parte Vieja |
| **La Cuchara de San Telmo** | Modern pintxos | San Telmo |
| **Berasategui** | Traditional | Old Town |
| **Txepetxa** | Anchovies | Parte Vieja |
| **Bar Antonio** | Mushroom pintxos | Parte Vieja |
| **A fuego negro** | Creative | Old Town |

### Best Restaurants (Sit-Down)

| Restaurant | Style | Stars | Notes |
|------------|-------|-------|-------|
| **Arzak** | Molecular | 3★ | Landmark |
| **Martín Berasategui** | Modern | 3★ | Lasarte-Oria (30 min) |
| **Mugaritz** | Creative | 2★ | San Sebastián |
| **Etxea** | Traditional | 1★ | Best value |

### Must-See / Do

- **La Concha walk** — Promenade, beach, views
- **Monte Igueldo** — Funicular, panoramas
- **San Telmo Museum** — Basque culture
- **Mercado de la Bretxa** — Morning market
- **Sunset at Monte Igueldo** — Best views

### Beach

- **La Concha**: Urban, safe, iconic
- **Ondarreta**: Near Miramar Palace
- **Zurriola**: Surf beach, younger crowd

## Bilbao

### The Drive

- **From Hossegor**: 1h 45min / 160 km
- **Route**: A63 → A8 (E70) → Bilbao
- **Parking**: Guggenheim area, Old Town

### Best for

- **Guggenheim** — Iconic architecture
- **Casco Viejo** — Old town, bars
- **Museums** — Fine Arts, Basque
- **Shopping** — Fashion

### Must-See / Do

- **Guggenheim Bilbao** — Frank Gehry landmark
- **Casco Viejo** — 7 streets, pintxos
- **Bilbao Fine Arts Museum** — Top collection
- **Zubizuri** — Santiago Calatrava bridge
- **Mercado de la Ribera** — Food market

### Best Pintxo Bars (Bilbao)

| Bar | Specialty |

| **La Viña del Puerto** | Pintxos |
| **Txoko** | Club-style |

**Bilbao + Guggenheim + pintxos in Casco Viejo = full day**

## Hondarribia (Fuenterrabia)

### The Drive

- **From Hossegor**: 25 min / 35 km
- **Closest to border**

### Best for

- **Old Town** — Fortified walls
- **Beach** — Hondarribia beach

### Must-See

- **Castillo de Carlos V** — Fortress

## Border Crossing Tips

- **No passport** needed (EU)
- **E70 toll**: ~€15 each way
- **Best time**: Early morning or after 3pm
- **Avoid**: Friday pm, Sunday pm (traffic)
