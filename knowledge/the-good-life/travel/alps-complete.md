# French Alps — Complete Guide

Comprehensive guide to the French Alps. Use when user asks about **Alps**, **ski**, **Courchevel**, **Méribel**, **Chamonix**, **Alps**, or **French Alps**.

---

## Overview

The French Alps are the most famous ski destination in the world. From glamorous Courchevel to classic Chamonix, the Alps offer world-class skiing, stunning scenery, and alpine charm.

**Key Areas**:
- **Tarentaise** (Three Valleys): Courchevel, Méribel, Val Thorens
- **Mont Blanc**: Chamonix, Megève, Saint-Gervais
- **Maurienne**: Valmeinier, Les Menuires, Saint-Martin-de-Belleville
- **Savoie**: Albertville, Chambéry

---

## The Big Three (Three Valleys)

### Courchevel — The Glamour

**The Resort**
- **Location**: Savoie
- **Elevation**: 1,738m (village) - 2,738m (top)
- **Runs**: 150+
- **Vibe**: Most glamorous, expensive, star-studded

**What Makes It Special**
- Most famous resort
- Luxury hotels, shops, restaurants
- Largest ski area (600km+)
- Heli-skiing
- Michelin-starred restaurants on mountain

**The Villages**

### Méribel — The Heart

**The Resort**
- **Location**: Savoie (center of Three Valleys)
- **Elevation**: 1,400m - 2,952m
- **Runs**: 150+
- **Vibe**: Family-friendly, British influence, charming

**What Makes It Special**

### Val Thorens — The High

**The Resort**
- **Location**: Savoie
- **Elevation**: 2,300m (highest ski resort in Europe)
- **Runs**: 140+
- **Vibe**: Party, young, highest

**What Makes It Special**
- Highest ski resort in Europe
- Great snow reliability
- Party scene

## Chamonix — The Classic

**The Town**
- **Population**: 10,000
- **Location**: Haute-Savoie
- **Vibe**: Alpine capital, mountaineering history, serious ski area

**What Makes It Special**
- Home of extreme skiing
- Mont Blanc views
- Historic alpine town
- Best off-piste in France
-not just skiing — climbing, hiking

## Other Major Resorts

### Megève — The Elegant

- **Location**: Haute-Savoie
- **Vibe**: aristocratic, family-friendly, less known internationally
- **Hotels**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Properties

### Saint-Gervais — The Thermal

- **Location**: Near Chamonix
- **Special**: Thermal spa (Les Thermes)
- **Vibe**: Underrated, authentic

### Les Menuires — The Value

- **Location**: Savoie
- **Vibe**: Purpose-built, family-friendly
- **Best**: Value in Three Valleys

### Tignes — The High

- **Location**: Savoie
- **Elevation**: 2,100m - 3,450m
- **Special**: Year-round skiing (glacier)
- **Vibe**: Young, party, serious skiers

## Ski Tips

1. **Book early** — Accomms book months ahead
2. **Rent locally** — Better than bringing
3. **Get insurance** — Ski insurance recommended
4. **Hire instructor** — For off-piste in Chamonix
5. **Watch weather** — Conditions change fast

---

## Kelly's Alps Advice

1. **For the experience** — Courchevel (glamour)
2. **For skiing** — Chamonix (serious)
3. **For families** — Méribel (charming)
4. **For snow** — Tignes or Val Thorens
5. **Don't overthink** — Any resort is great
