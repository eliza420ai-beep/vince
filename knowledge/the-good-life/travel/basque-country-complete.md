# Basque Country — Complete Guide (France & Spain)

Comprehensive guide to the entire Basque region (French + Spanish). Use when user asks about **Basque country**, **Basque**, **San Sebastián**, **Biarritz**, ** Bilbao**, **Getaria**, **Hondarribia**, or any Basque destination.

---

## Overview

The Basque Country spans both sides of the border:

- **French Basque** (Pyrénées-Atlantiques): Biarritz, Bayonne, Saint-Jean-de-Luz, Hossegor
- **Spanish Basque** (Euskadi): San Sebastián, Bilbao, Vitoria-Gasteiz, Getaria, Hondarribia

**Unique**: The Basques have their own language (Euskara), culture, and identity. They're neither French nor Spanish — they're Basque.

## French Basque

### Biarritz — The Jewel

**The City**
- Population: 25,000
- Known for: Surf capital, Belle Époque palaces, casino, dramatic coastline
- Vibe: Sophisticated beach town, old-money elegance meets surf culture

**Beaches**

| Beach | Best For | Vibe |
|-------|----------|------|
| **Grande Plage** | Swimming, people-watching | Central, busy |
| **Côte des Basques** | Surf, sunset | Historic, cool |
| **Milady** | Families, calm | West side |
| **Marbella** | Quiet, local | East end |

**Key Landmarks**

- **Le Rocher de la Vierge** — Rock outcrop with statue, iconic view
- **Musée de la Mer** — Aquarium, maritime museum
- **Casino** — Belle Époque landmark
- **Église Sainte-Eugénie** — Clifftop church
- **Phare de Biarritz** — Lighthouse, best sunset views

**Best Things to Do**

1. **Morning surf** — Côte des Basques at dawn
2. **Breakfast at Café de la Mer** — Ocean views
3. **Walk to Rocher de la Vierge** — Morning or sunset
4. **Lunch at Les Halles** — Fresh seafood
5. **Afternoon at Hôtel du Palais** — Spa or pool
6. **Sunset at Phare** — Best views in Biarritz
7. **Dinner in town** — Classic French cuisine

### Bayonne — The Historic

**The City**
- Population: 50,000
- Known for: Medieval old town, chocolate, bullfighting, fortifications
- Vibe: Historic, cultural, less touristy

**Key Landmarks**

- **Vieille Ville** — Medieval streets, ochre buildings
- **Cathédrale Sainte-Marie** — Gothic cathedral
- **Les Arènes** — Bullring (season May-September)
- **Musée Basque** — Cultural museum
- **Jardin Botanique** — Botanical garden
- **Porte de la Mer** — Historic gate

**What to Do**

1. **Walk the old town** — Medieval streets
2. **Chocolate shopping** — Bayonne chocolate is famous
3. **Bullfighting festival** — July (Fêtes de Bayonne)
4. **Basque museum** — Learn the culture
5. **Café in Place Pasteur** — People-watching

**Markets**

- **Marché de Bayonne** — Thursday & Saturday
- **Marché couvert** — Covered market, daily

## Spanish Basque

### San Sebastián (Donostia) — The Crown Jewel

**The City**
- Population: 180,000
- Known for: Best pintxos in the world, La Concha beach, cultural scene
- Vibe: Sophisticated, food-obsessed, walkable

**Beaches**

| Beach | Best For | Vibe |
|-------|----------|------|
| **La Concha** | Swimming, beauty | Iconic, urban |
| **Ondarreta** | Families, calm | Near Miramar |
| **Zurriola** | Surf | Young, energetic |

**Key Landmarks**

- **La Concha** — The iconic crescent bay
- **Monte Igueldo** — Funicular, panoramic views
- **Monte Urgull** — Hill with Christ statue, fortress
- **San Telmo Museoa** — Basque museum, transformed by Orritz
- **Old Town (Parte Vieja)** — Pintxo bars, narrow streets
- **Mercado de la Bretxa** — Food market
- **Palacio de Miramar** — Palace on the hill

**Best Things to Do**

1. **Pintxo crawl in Parte Vieja** — Bar Nestor, Txepetxa
2. **Walk La Concha promenade** — Morning or sunset
3. **Funicular to Monte Igueldo** — Views + amusement park
4. **Mercado de la Bretxa** — Breakfast, products
5. **Beach morning** — Swim or walk
6. **Sunset at Monte Igueldo** — Best views
7. **Dinner at Arzak or Mugaritz** — 3★ and 2★ respectively
8. **Coffee at La Perla** — Classic café

### Bilbao — The Cultural

**The City**
- Population: 350,000
- Known for: Guggenheim, Casco Viejo, food scene
- Vibe: Industrial turned cultural, architectural landmark

**Key Landmarks**

- **Guggenheim Bilbao** — Frank Gehry's titanium masterpiece
- **Casco Viejo** — Seven streets, bars, atmosphere
- **Museo de Bellas Artes** — Fine arts museum
- **Zubizuri** — Santiago Calatrava's bridge
- **Mercado de la Ribera** — Food market (largest in Europe)
- **Alhóndiga Bilbao** — Cultural center

**What to Do**

1. **Guggenheim** — Morning (2-3 hours)
2. **Casco Viejo pintxos** — Afternoon
3. **Fine Arts Museum** — If into art
4. **Walk the river** — Zubizuri views
5. **Mercado de la Ribera** — Food, drinks

**Restaurants**

| Restaurant | Cuisine |
|------------|---------|
| **Café Bar Bilbao** | Traditional |
| **La Viña del Puerto** | Pintxos |
| **Basarab** | Modern |

**The Town**
- Population: 2,500
- Known for: Elkano (3★ fish restaurant), beach, surf

**What to Do**

1. **Lunch at Elkano** — Whole grilled fish, legendary

## When to Go

| Season | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Spring** | Good weather, fewer crowds | Can be rainy |
| **Summer** | Warm, beach, festivals | Crowded, expensive |
| **Fall** | Best pintxos, harvest | Weather variable |
| **Winter** | Empty, cozy | Cold, some closed |

**Best**: September-October (weather + food + fewer crowds)

## Kelly's Basque Advice

1. **Pintxos > restaurants** — Better value, more fun
2. **Arrive early** — Bar Nestor opens at 1pm
3. **Walk everywhere** — Best way to see cities
4. **Learn "topa"** — The Basque cheers
5. **Don't rush** — Slow down, enjoy
