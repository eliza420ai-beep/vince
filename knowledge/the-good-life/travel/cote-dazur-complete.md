# Côte d'Azur — Complete Guide (French Riviera)

Comprehensive guide to the French Riviera. Use when user asks about **Côte d'Azur**, **French Riviera**, **Nice**, **Cannes**, **Monaco**, **Saint-Tropez**, **Antibes**, **Menton**, or **Riviera**.

## Overview

The Côte d'Azur (French Riviera) is the most famous coastline in Europe. 550km of Mediterranean coast from Menton to Cassis, starring glitz, glamour, art, and sun.

**Key Areas**:
- **East**: Monaco, Menton, Nice, Villefranche-sur-Mer
- **Center**: Antibes, Cannes, Grasse
- **West**: Saint-Tropez, Sainte-Maxime, Cassis

**Best for**: Beaches, yachts, art, history, dining, shopping

## Monaco — The Principality

**The Basics**
- **Population**: 38,000
- **Area**: 2 km² (smallest country)
- **Vibe**: Ultimate luxury, casino, yachts, F1

**Key Landmarks**

- **Palais Princier** — Prince's palace
- **Cathédrale de Monaco** — Where Grace Kelly married

- **Oceanographic Museum** — Iconic building

**What to Do**

1. **Casino** — Even just to look
