# Pyrénées — Complete Guide

Comprehensive guide to the French and Spanish Pyrénées. Use when user asks about **Pyrénées**, **ski**, **mountains**, **Andorra**, ** Gavarnie**, ** Cirque de So**, or **Pyrenees hiking**.

---

## Overview

The Pyrénées mountain range spans the border between France and Spain, from the Atlantic to the Mediterranean. 430km of mountains, with peaks over 3,000m.

**Key Areas**:
- **Western Pyrénées** (Haute-Garonne, Pyrénées-Atlantiques): Near Toulouse, Basque Country
- **Central Pyrénées** (Hautes-Pyrénées, Ariège): Big mountains, skiing, iconic cirques
- **Eastern Pyrénées** (Pyrénées-Orientales): Catalonia, Mediterranean feel

#### Gourette — The Closest French

- **Location**: Near Pau, Vallée d' Ossau
- **Elevation**: 1,350m (base) - 2,425m (top)
- **Runs**: 40+ (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- **Lifts**: 17
- **Snow**: Good, north-facing

**What Makes It Special**
- Closest major French resort to Hossegor
- Traditional mountain village
- Good variety of terrain

**Facilities**
- Ski school
- Equipment rental
- Restaurants on mountain
- Parking at base

#### Artouste — The Scenic

- **Location**: Vallée d' Ossau
- **Elevation**: 1,550m - 2,400m
- **Runs**: 20+
- **Character**: Smaller, quieter, stunning views

**What Makes It Special**
- Less crowded
- Beautiful valley setting
- Good for beginners

#### Candanchú (Spain) — The Historic

- **Location**: Aragón, Spain
- **Elevation**: 1,500m - 2,100m
- **Runs**: 40+
- **Character**: Oldest ski resort in Spain, reliable

**What Makes It Special**
- Historic (opened 1928)
- Reliable snow
- Less crowded than French resorts

#### Formigal (Spain) — The Big One

- **Location**: Huesca, Aragón
- **Elevation**: 1,500m - 2,250m
- **Runs**: 90+
- **Lifts**: 20+
- **Character**: Largest in Aragón, modern

**What Makes It Special**
- Most runs
- Modern facilities
- Best terrain variety

### Summer: Hiking & Villages

#### Cirque de Gavarnie (UNESCO)

- **Location**: Hautes-Pyrénées
- **Drive from Hossegor**: 2h 30min
- **What**: The most famous cirque (amphitheater) in the Pyrénées
- **Elevation**: 1,350m (village) - 1,650m (cirque floor)
- **Hike**: Easy walk from village to cirque (30 min)

**What to See**
- Grand Cirque — 1,400m walls
- Grande Cascade — 423m waterfall
- Village of Gavarnie — Traditional stone houses

**Nearby**
- Cirque de Troumouse — Even bigger, less visited
- Pont de Neige — Winter-only bridge

#### Cirque d'Estaubé

- **Location**: Near Gavarnie
- **Character**: Smaller, quieter
- **Hike**: 1 hour circuit

### Key Towns

#### Lourdes

- **Population**: 15,000
- **Known for**: Pilgrimage site, Marian apparitions (1858)
- **Vibe**: Religious tourism, but also charming old town
- **Drive from Hossegor**: 2h

**What to See**
- **Sanctuaires Notre-Dame** — Huge pilgrimage church
- **Grotto of Massabielle** — Where apparitions occurred
- **Old town** — Fortified city
- **Castelnaudary** — Not here, wrong direction

**If Passing Through**
- Quick stop at the sanctuaries
- Don't need much time

#### Pau

- **Population**: 80,000
- **Known for**: Castle, view of mountains, English heritage
- **Vibe**: Regional capital, elegant
- **Drive from Hossegor**: 1h 30min

**What to See**
- **Château de Pau** — Birthplace of Henry IV
- ** Boulevard des Pyrénées** — Best view of mountains
- **Musée des Beaux-Arts** — Art museum
- **Funiculaire** — Up to castle

**If You Need**

## Driving the Pyrénées

### Key Routes

#### Route des Pyrénées (D918)

- **What**: The classic mountain road
- **From**: Pau → Tarbes → Lourdes → Gavarnie
- **Character**: Scenic, passes, villages

#### Col du Tourmalet

- **Elevation**: 2,115m
- **Route**: Barèges → Luz-Saint-Sauveur
- **Character**: Iconic Tour de France climb
- **Summer**: Open, spectacular
- **Winter**: Closed

#### Col d'Aubisque

- **Elevation**: 1,509m
- **Route**: Near Laruns
- **Character**: Another Tour classic

#### Tunnel du Somport (France→Spain)

- **Location**: Near Candanchú
- **What**: Road tunnel under mountains
- **Alternative**: Col du Somport (open summer)
