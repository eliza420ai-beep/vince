## Weekend Option 1: San Sebastián Pintxo Pilgrimage

**Friday Night**
- Drive to San Sebastián (30 min)
- Dinner: Pintxos in Parte Vieja
- Walk: La Concha at night
- Stay: Hotel Maria Christina or similar

**Saturday**
- 9am: Coffee + pastry at La Perla
- 11am: Walk Monte Igueldo
- 1pm: Pintxos crawl (Bar Nestor, Txepetxa)
- 3pm: Museum or beach
- 6pm: Aperitif at La Perla
- 8pm: Dinner at Arzak or Mugaritz (book ahead)

**Sunday**
- 9am: Mercado de la Bretxa
- 11am: Drive to Getaria (20 min)
- 12pm: Lunch at Elkano (grilled fish)
- 2pm: Drive home (45 min)

## Booking Tips

- **Restaurants**: Book 48h+ ahead for Saturday dinner
- **Spas**: Book treatments in advance
- **Hotels**: Saturday nights = premium
- **Golf**: Tee times 8am best

---

## Kelly's Weekend Advice

1. **Saturday = activity day** — museums, golf, spa, market
2. **Sunday = lazy** — beach, brunch, home
3. **Book ahead** — especially restaurants and spas
4. **Leave early** — beat the traffic, enjoy the morning
5. **Always have a backup** — weather happens
