# French Wine — Region by Region

Quick reference for French wine. Use when user asks for **French wine**, **wine from France**, **what wine to drink**, or needs **wine recommendation**.

---

## The Regions

### Bordeaux — The Icon

**Red**: Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot blends
**White**: Sauvignon Blanc, Sémillon

**Key areas:**
- **Left Bank** (Médoc): Cabernet-dominant, structured, aging potential
- **Right Bank** (Saint-Émilion, Pomerol): Merlot-dominant, softer, earlier drinking

**What to drink:**
| Style | Price | Producers |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Everyday | €15-30 | Lynch-Bages, Angludet, Giscours |
| Special | €40-80 | Margaux, Latour, Lafite, Mouton |
| Splurge | €100+ | Pétrus, Le Pin, Lafleur |

**Food pairing**: Red = beef, lamb, duck. White = seafood, white fish.

---

### Burgundy — The Elegance

**Red**: Pinot Noir
**White**: Chardonnay

**Key areas:**
- **Côte de Nuits**: Pinot Noir (Vosne-Romanée, Chambolle-Musigny)
- **Côte de Beaune**: White (Puligny, Chassagne, Meursault)

**What to drink:**
| Style | Price | Producers |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Village | €25-50 | Armand Rousseau, Dugat-Py |
| Premier Cru | €50-150 | DRC, Leroy |
| Grand Cru | €150+ | Romanee-Conti, Musigny |

**Food pairing**: Red = duck, chicken, mushroom. White = lobster, cream sauces, chicken.

---

### Champagne — The Celebration

**Grapes**: Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Pinot Meunier

**Producers:**
- **Houses**: Veuve Clicquot, Moët & Chandon, Ruinart, Bollinger
- **Growers**: Jacques Selosse, Vouette & Sorbée, Billecart-Salmon

**Styles:**
- **Brut**: Most common, dry
- **Rosé**: Red fruit, salmon color
- **Vintage**: Single year, aged
- **Prestige**: Dom Pérignon, Cristal

**Food pairing**: Oysters, caviar, fried foods, celebration.

---

### Rhône — The Power

**Red**: Grenache, Syrah, Mourvèdre
**White**: Viognier, Roussanne, Marsanne

**Key areas:**
- **Northern** (Hermitage, Côte-Rôtie): Syrah, structured
- **Southern** (Châteauneuf-du-Pape): Grenache blends, rich

**What to drink:**
| Style | Price | Producers |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Côtes du Rhône | €15-25 | Jaboulet, Guigal |
| Villages | €25-40 | Chave, Jaboulet |
| Hermitage | €50-150 | Chave, Jaboulet, Belle |

**Food pairing**: Red = lamb, game, stew. White = rich fish, creamy dishes.

---

### Loire — The Versatility

**Grapes**: Sauvignon Blanc, Chenin Blanc, Cabernet Franc, Melon de Bourgogne

**Key areas:**
- **Sancerre**: Sauvignon Blanc, crisp
- **Pouilly-Fumé**: Sauvignon Blanc, smoky
- **Vouvray**: Chenin, sparkling and dry
- **Chinon/Bourgueil**: Cabernet Franc, light red

**Food pairing**: Sancerre = goat cheese, seafood. Chinon = chicken, light meat.

---

### Alsace — The Character

**Grapes**: Gewürztraminer, Riesling, Pinot Gris, Gewürztraminer

**Key areas:**
- **Riesling**: Dry, mineral, food-friendly
- **Gewürztraminer**: Aromatic, off-dry
- **Pinot Gris**: Rich, weighty

**Food pairing**: Riesling = seafood, Asian. Gewürztraminer = foie gras, strong cheese.

---

### Provence — The Summer

**Grapes**: Grenache, Cinsault, Mourvèdre (Rosé)

**Key areas:**
- **Bandol**: More structured, aging potential
- **Cassis**: Crisp, mineral
- **Standard Provence**: Light, refreshing

**Food pairing**: Rosé = summer, pool, light food, salads.

---

### Southwest — The Hidden Gems

**Key areas:**
- **Madiran**: Tannat, powerful reds
- **Cahors**: Malbec, tannic
- **Jurançon**: Sweet Chenin, aperitif
- **Pacherenc**: Dry Chenin

**Food pairing**: Madiran = duck confit, cheese. Cahors = beef, game.

## Quick Decision Guide

| Situation | Wine |
|----------|------|
| **Special occasion** | Burgundy (DRC) or Champagne |
| **Dinner party red** | Bordeaux (Margaux, Pauillac) |
| **Summer afternoon** | Provence Rosé |
| **Seafood lunch** | Chablis or Sancerre |
| **Cold night** | Rhône (Hermitage) or Cahors |
| **Celebration** | Champagne (Vintage or Grower) |
| **Cheese course** | Loire (Sauvignon) or Alsace (Gewürztraminer) |
| **Everyday** | Côtes du Rhône, Bordeaux everyday |

## Kelly's French Wine Routine

1. **Ask what they're eating** — food is the guide
2. **Match intensity** — light wine with light food, bold with bold
3. **Default to region** — match the cuisine (Italian → Italian wine)
4. **When in doubt** — Champagne or Bordeaux
5. **Never overthink** — drink what you enjoy
