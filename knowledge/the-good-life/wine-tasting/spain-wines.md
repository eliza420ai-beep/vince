# Spanish Wine Guide

Comprehensive guide to Spanish wine for Kelly. Use when user asks for **Spanish wine**, **wine from Spain**, **Rioja**, **Ribera del Duero**, **Priorat**, **San Sebastián wine**, or **pairing with Spanish food**.

---

## Key Regions

### Rioja — The Classic

**Rioja Reserva**

- **Producer picks**:
  - La Rioja Alta (904, 890)
  - Marqués de Riscal
  - Marqués de Murrieta
  - CVNE (Compañía Vinícola del Norte de España)
  - Muga
  - Abel Hernández
  - Sierra Cantabria
  - Artadi (villages-focused)

**Tasting notes**: Vanilla, cherry, leather, tobacco, cedar. Oak aging is signature.

**Drinking window**: 5-15 years for reservas

**Rioja Gran Reserva**

- **Producer picks**:
  - La Rioja Alta 890
  - Marqués de Murrieta Castillo Ygay
  - CVNE Imperial
  - Muga Prado Enea

**Tasting notes**: Developed, tertiary notes (leather, earth), smooth, elegant.

**Drinking window**: 15-30 years

**Rioja white**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Marqués de Murrieta Capellanía
  - La Rioja Alta 924
  - Remelluri

**Tasting notes**: Apple, pear, vanilla, richness.

---

### Ribera del Duero — The Power

**Top Producers**

- **Vega Sicilia** (the legend)
  - Único (10+ years aging)
  - Valbuena
  - Alión
  - Pintia
- **Pingus** (world's most expensive wine)
- **Flor de Pingus**
- **Dominio de Pingus**
- **Emilio Moro**
- **Aalto**
- **Tinto Pesquera**
- **Protos**
- **Concha y Toro** (Don Melchor)

**Tasting notes**: Black fruit, blackberry, spice, oak, structured. Big tannins, long aging potential.

**Drinking window**: 10-30 years for top wines

---

### Priorat — The Intensity

**Producer picks**

- **Clos Mogador** (the pioneer)
- **L'Ermita** (most expensive)
- **Costers del Siurana**
- **Álvaro Palacios** (founder)
- **Ferrer Bobet**
- **Cims de Porrera**

**Tasting notes**: Black fruit, minerals, liquorice, intensity. Garnacha + Cariñena.

**Drinking window**: 10-25 years

---

### Rías Baixas — Albariño

**Producer picks**

- **Pazo de Señoráns**
- **Bodegas del Palacio**
- **Fillaboa**
- **Martín Codax**
- **Senora de Rosario**

**Tasting notes**: Citrus, peach, saline, mineral. Crisp, refreshing.

**Best for**: Seafood, fish, oysters, tapas.

---

### Sherry & Montilla

**Jerez (Sherry)**

- **Fino**: Manzanilla (Sanlúcar)
- **Amontillado**: Nutty, dry
- **Oloroso**: Rich, dry
- **PX (Pedro Ximénez)**: Sweet, dessert

**Producer picks**:
- Bodegas Osborne
- Bodegas Williams & Humbert
- Álvaro Domecq
- La Guita

**Montilla-Moriles**

- Similar to sherry but from Montilla region
- Often better value

---

### Other Regions

**Ribeira Sacra** (Galicia)

- **Producer picks**:
  - Dominio do Bibei
  - Guímaro
  - Fedellos do Couto

**Tasting notes**: Mencía grape. Light to medium bodied, mineral, fresh.

**Rueda**

- **Producer picks**:
  - José Pariente
  - Aura
  - Belondrade

**Tasting notes**: Verdejo grape. Crisp, apple, herbal.

**Txakoli** (Basque)

- **Producer picks**:
  - Txomin Etxaniz
  - Ameztoi

**Tasting notes**: Tart, sparkling, refreshing. For tapas.

## Spanish Wine Service

### Temperature

| Type | Temperature |
|------|-------------|
| Txakoli | 6-8°C |
| Albariño | 8-10°C |
| Fino/Manzanilla | 8-10°C |
| White Rioja | 10-12°C |
| Light red (Mencía) | 14-16°C |
| Reserva | 16-18°C |
| Gran Reserva | 16-18°C |
| Ribera/Priorat | 16-18°C |
| PX Sherry | 10-12°C |

### Glassware

- **Rioja**: Large Burgundy glass
- **Ribera del Duero**: Bordeaux glass
- **Albariño**: White wine glass
- **Sherry**: Small Sherry copita

### Decanting

- **Rioja Gran Reserva**: 1-2 hours
- **Ribera del Duero**: 1-2 hours for young wines
- **Priorat**: 1-2 hours

## San Sebastián & Basque Country

### Local Wines

**Txakoli** (white, slightly sparkling)

- **Best**: Getaria, Zarautz
- **Producer**: Ameztoi, Txomin Etxaniz
- **Pairing**: Pintxos, seafood

**Rioja Alavesa**

- **Producer**: Viñedos de Páganos, Finca la Embutina
- **Style**: Modern, fruit-forward

### San Sebastián Pintxo Wine Pairing

| Pintxo | Wine |
|--------|------|
| Gilda (olive, anchovy, guindilla) | Txakoli |
| Jamón croquette | Rioja |
| Grilled octopus | Albariño |
| Beef tenderloin | Rioja Reserva |
| Salt cod | Fino |

3. **Service**: Include temperature
4. **Alternative**: Show price tiers
5. **When in doubt**: Rioja Reserva or Ribera del Duero
