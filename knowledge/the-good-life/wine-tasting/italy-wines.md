# Italian Wine Guide

Comprehensive guide to Italian wine for Kelly. Use when user asks for **Italian wine**, **wine from Italy**, **Barolo**, **Barbaresco**, **Chianti**, **Super Tuscan**, **Amarone**, or **pairing with Italian food**.

---

## Key Regions

### Piedmont — The King

**Barolo** (King of wines)

- **Villages**: Barolo, Monforte d'Alba, Castiglione Falletto, Serralunga d'Alba
- **Producer picks**:
  - Giacomo Conterno (Monfortino Riserva — 100pt legendary)
  - Bruno Giacosa (fallettoRiserva)
  - Giuseppe Mascarello
  - Bartolo Mascarello
  - Roberto Voerzio
  - Luciano Sandrone
  - Paolo Scavino
  - Giacomo Borgogno

**Tasting notes**: Tar, roses, dried cherries, truffle, tar, leather. Full-bodied, tannins need time.

**Drinking window**: 10-30 years for top wines

**Barbaresco** (Queen)

- **Villages**: Barbaresco, Neive, Treiso
- **Producer picks**:
  - Gaja (most famous)
  - Bruno Giacosa
  - Produttori del Barbaresco (cooperative, excellent value)
  - Marchesi di Grésy
  - La Spinetta

**Tasting notes**: Roses, red cherries, violets, tar. Lighter than Barolo but equally elegant.

**Drinking window**: 8-25 years

**Barbera d'Alba**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Giacomo Conterno
  - Bruno Giacosa
  - Paolo Scavino

**Tasting notes**: Cherries, plums, spice. More approachable, earlier drinking.

**Best value**: ~€20-50 for exceptional quality

**Dolcetto**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Giacomo Conterno
  - Bruno Giacosa

**Tasting notes**: Sweet fruit, plums, easy-drinking. Drink young (3-5 years).

### Tuscany — Super Tuscans & Chianti

**Brunello di Montalcino**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Biondi-Santi (original, 100+ years)
  - Case Basse (Gianfranco Soldera)
  - Poggio di Sotto
  - Fuligni
  - Il Poggione
  - San Giuseppe (Stella di Campriano)
  - Lisini

**Tasting notes**: Cherry, leather, earth, tobacco. Full-bodied, long aging.

**Drinking window**: 10-30 years

**Vino Nobile di Montepulciano**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Poliziano
  - Avignonesi
  - Fassati

**Tasting notes**: Similar to Brunello but lighter

**Super Tuscans**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Sassicaia (Tenuta San Guido) — the original
  - Tignanello (Antinori)
  - Solaia (Antinori)
  - Ornellaia
  - Masseto
  - Guado al Tasso (Antinori)
  - Fontodi
  - Isole e Olena
  - Fattoria di Petrolo

**Tasting notes**: Cabernet Sauvignon + Sangiovese blends. Blackcurrant, cedar, elegance.

**Drinking window**: 10-25 years

**Chianti Classico**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Isole e Olena
  - Fontodi
  - Castello di Ama
  - Fattoria di Maiano
  - Rocca di Montegrossi

**Tasting notes**: Cherry, herbs, earth, acidity. Sangiovese at its purest.

---

### Veneto — Amarone & Prosecco

**Amarone della Valpolicella**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Giuseppe Quintarelli (legendary, rare)
  - Dal Forno Romano
  - Masi
  - Tommasi
  - Zenato
  - Allegrini

**Tasting notes**: Dried fruit, chocolate, spices, full-bodied, high alcohol (16%+). Rich, opulent.

**Drinking window**: 15-40 years

**Valpolicella Ripasso**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Masi
  - Tommasi
  - Allegrini

**Tasting notes**: Lighter than Amarone, cherries, herbs. Better value.

**Prosecco (Superiore DOCG)**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Bortolomiol
  - Nino Franco
  - Carpenè Malvolti
  - NINO FRANCO

**Tasting notes**: Green apple, pear, floral. Lighter, sparkling.

---

### Trentino-Alto Adige

**Trentino**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Ferrari (Trento method sparkling)
  - Maso Cantanghel

**Tasting notes**: Apples, citrus, crisp. Excellent sparkling.

**Alto Adige (Südtirol)**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Elena Walch
  - Hofstätter
  - Tenuta San Leonardo
  - Arunda

**Tasting notes**: Alpine freshness, apples, herbs. Best white wines in Italy.

### Other Regions

**Sicily**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Donnafugata
  - Tenuta di Contessa Entellina
  - Planeta
  - COS

**Tasting notes**: Nero d'Avola, Grillo. Mediterranean, sun, complexity.

**Sardinia**

- **Producer picks**:
  - Argiolas

**Tasting notes**: Cannonau (Grenache), Vermentino. Mediterranean, earthy.

## Italian Wine Service

### Temperature

| Type | Temperature |
|------|-------------|
| Sparkling (Prosecco) | 6-8°C |
| Light white | 8-10°C |
| Full white (oaked) | 12-14°C |
| Light red (Pinot Nero) | 14-16°C |
| Medium red (Chianti, Barbera) | 16-18°C |
| Full red (Barolo, Brunello) | 18-20°C |
| Amarone | 18-20°C |

### Glassware

- **Barolo/Brunello**: Large Burgundy glass
- **Chianti**: Standard red glass
- **Prosecco**: Flute (or white wine glass for vintage)
- **Amarone**: Large Bordeaux glass

### Decanting

- **Barolo**: 1-2 hours for young wines
- **Brunello**: 2-3 hours
- **Amarone**: 1-2 hours
- **Riserva**: Always decant

## Kelly's Italian Wine Routine

When the user asks for Italian wine:

1. **Default**: Suggest by region and occasion
2. **Pairing**: Match to the food they'll eat
3. **Service**: Include temperature and glassware
4. **Value**: Suggest alternatives at different price points
5. **When in doubt**: Barolo or Brunello is always safe
