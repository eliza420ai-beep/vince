## The Next Bull Market Will Be Built on These Sectors. Here's the Full Playbook.

**Our 100-stock bench, 13 sectors deep, with the scorecard data and bull cases that map the entire AI infrastructure trade.**

---

Most investors are still looking at AI through the wrong lens. They see ChatGPT, they see Copilot, they see a chatbot — and they think they understand the trade.

They don't.

$650–700 billion in hyperscaler capex is being deployed in 2026 alone. That's Switzerland's GDP — in a single year, from four companies. Roughly 75% goes directly into physical infrastructure. Not apps. Buildings, chips, cables, cooling systems, and power plants.

Revenue flows up. Capital flows down. The consumer sees the product. The investor sees the supply chain. The best investors see the supply chain before the product ships.

What follows is our full sector-by-sector playbook — every layer of the AI stack, plus the adjacent sectors (space, defence, materials, digital finance, digital health) that we believe will define the next decade of returns. ## I. AI Semiconductors

**The layer that prints money.**

This is where the highest margins and deepest concentration in the entire AI economy live. NVIDIA holds ~92% of the AI data centre GPU market. TSMC manufactures nearly 70% of the world's advanced chips. ASML is literally the only supplier of EUV lithography machines on the planet.

One company designs. One company builds. One company makes the machine that builds. That is both an investment thesis and a geopolitical risk.

**$NVDA** — Full-year FY2026 revenue: $215.9B, up 65% YoY. Data centre Q4 revenue: $62.3B, up 75% YoY. Q1 FY2027 guidance: $78B (crushed the $72.6B consensus). Blackwell revenue projected to jump from ~$7B to $93.7B this year. Gross margins: mid-70s. Cash: $62.5B. At $115 (25% off ATH), consensus PT of $175 implies **+52% upside**. Scorecard composite: **66.1** — #6 on the bench.

**$TSM** — Nearly 70% foundry market share. Samsung is at 7.2%. At $338, PT $429 implies +27%. Composite: **71.0** — #2 on the bench, with the cleanest flag profile (high momentum, no insider selling).

**$AVGO** — The custom ASIC king for Google, Meta, and others. VMware acquisition generating margin expansion. At $321 (23% off ATH), PT $380 implies +18%. Composite: 51.3 — flagged for high P/E and 40%+ drawdown risk.

**$MU** — Entire calendar 2026 HBM production sold out. Q1 FY2026 revenue: record $13.6B, up 57% YoY. Gross margins: 57%. HBM TAM forecast: $100B by 2028 (pulled forward two years). Q2 guidance: $18.7B revenue, 68% margins. At $104 (34% off ATH), PT $150 implies **+44%**. Composite: **64.7** — #7 on the bench.

**The rest of the semis bench:** $AMD ($192, PT $190, flat — fairly valued), $MRVL ($90, PT $135, +50% — see Networking below), $ARM ($117, PT $170, +45% — but flagged pe_above_50_low_growth), $CRDO ($58, PT $75, +29% — energy-efficient data transfer pure-play whose stock rose 117% in 2025).

**Equipment makers** are critical and often overlooked: $AMAT ($325, PT $425, +31%), $KLAC ($1,418, PT $1,750, +23%), $LRCX ($212, PT $275, +30%). These companies make the machines that make the chips. When TSMC expands capacity, these are the first cheques written.

**EDA/design tools:** $CDNS ($290, PT $380, +31%) and $SNPS ($520, PT $620, +19%) — the software that designs every chip before it's fabricated. Composites of 53.9 and 40.7 respectively; SNPS flagged for pe_above_50_low_growth.

**Wildcard:** $IONQ ($38, PT $65, +71%) — quantum computing. Composite: **70.0** — #4 on the bench, driven by a 97.1 earnings score. High momentum, 40%+ drawdown, insider selling. Pure optionality.

**Lead picks: $NVDA, $MU, $TSM.** The first two for upside, the third for quality.

## II. AI Storage

**The NAND supercycle nobody saw coming.**

**$SNDK** — SanDisk spun off from Western Digital in February 2025 and became the hottest stock in tech. Revenue grew 61% YoY. Gross margins expanded from 32% to 51%. EPS grew fivefold. Bernstein PT: $1,000. Analysts forecast FY2026 EPS of $22.96 — a 1,190% YoY jump.

Scorecard composite: **81.9 — the highest on the entire 100-stock bench.** Growth: 92.8. Earnings: 97.6. The insider selling flag is Western Digital liquidating its remaining stake, not management bailing.

## The Meta-Strategy

You don't have to play all six layers. Most investors should focus where the risk-reward is most asymmetric right now.

**If you buy five names and hold for 3 years:** $MU, $NVDA, $SNDK, $GEV, $MRVL. These are the core infrastructure picks with structural demand, proven earnings, and reasonable valuations. MU at 34% off ATH with HBM sold out is the cleanest setup. GEV at a 61.9 composite with EBITDA set to double is the most underappreciated.

**If you want growth-at-any-price:** $NBIS, $ONDS, $OKLO. Could 3–5x if they execute. Could halve on a bad quarter.

**If you want optionality:** $TSLA, $ASTS, $RKLB, $IONQ. Binary bets on the future. Call options with no expiration date.

**If you want contrarian value:** $SMCI (67% off ATH), $HIMS (70% off peak), $WDAY (52% off ATH, +74% upside), $ORCL (55% off ATH, +61% upside).

**If you want steady compounding:** $TSM, $CEG, $ETN. Nuclear + foundry + grid = decades of demand with the cleanest risk profiles on the bench.
